Android · Automatic replies

Your customers get an answer, even when you are busy.

ChatAssistant answers the people who write to your business on WhatsApp with the rules, texts, photos, videos, PDFs and voice notes you prepare. It runs on your own phone and keeps everything there: no servers, no accounts, no ads.

Coming soon toGoogle Play See how it works

Available for Android. It comes with the example content of a fictitious store, Kusi Accesorios, so you can try it right away and then replace it with your own.

What it does

Rules you write, replies that sound like you.

Your rules

Keywords (tolerant of typos), a welcome message, exact phrases you send, a fallback reply and follow-ups. Each rule can send text, photos, video, audio, voice notes and PDFs.

Stays on your phone

Rules, files, the activity log and the WhatsApp Web session are kept only in the app’s private storage. There are no servers, accounts, analytics or ads.

Looks after your number

It only replies to people who write to you, with natural delays, anti-spam pauses, a daily limit and automatic opt-out when someone writes “stop”.

How it works

Ready in three steps.

  1. Link your WhatsApp. Open WhatsApp Web inside the app and link it with a QR code or a pairing code, like any linked device.
  2. Prepare your replies. Edit the Kusi Accesorios examples or create your own rules, and try them in the simulator before going live.
  3. Turn the service on. It keeps answering in the background, with a visible notification from which you can stop it at any time.

2. What the app is

ChatAssistant is an independent tool that sends automatic replies in your one-to-one WhatsApp chats according to the rules you create: keywords, a welcome message, exact phrases that you send, a campaign reply to any message (paused per customer for the days you choose) and follow-ups. It works through WhatsApp Web, inside the app, as a linked device of your own account, on your own phone.

  • It is not a WhatsApp or Meta service and does not use the official WhatsApp Business Platform (API).
  • It only replies in chats where someone wrote to you, or where you sent one of your exact trigger phrases, and the follow-ups you set up only go to those same chats. It offers no bulk sending, broadcasts or messages to people who have not written to you.
  • It comes with example content from a fictitious store, “Kusi Accesorios”, so you can try it straight away. Replace it with your own information and files before turning the bot on with real customers.
  • It is free. If paid features are offered in the future, they will be sold through Google Play under its terms, and we will update these terms beforehand.

When you use ChatAssistant you agree to:

  • Reply only to people who write to you. Use the app to answer your own customers’ messages, not to start conversations with people who did not contact you.

  • Comply with WhatsApp’s terms. Respect WhatsApp’s Terms of Service and policies (including the WhatsApp Business policies when they apply to you) and the laws that apply to you, such as data protection, consumer protection and advertising laws.

  • Supervise the bot. Check your chats regularly. The app does not replace a person and may not reply in some situations (no connection, phone off, battery restrictions or changes in WhatsApp Web).
  • Act lawfully. Do not use the app to impersonate anyone, to deceive or harass people, or for any illegal purpose.
